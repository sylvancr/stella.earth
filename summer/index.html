Summer 2026

In July 2026, 150 artists, engineers, philosophers, entrepreneurs, and investors lived and worked at Stella for a month of open programming: 50 presentations, 10 performances, more than 3,000 meals, and one ventriloquist. Together they laid the foundations of what comes next — our first research labs, event series, and membership, beginning this year, and a fellowship cohort in autumn 2027.
